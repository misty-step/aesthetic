# Round 2 retention verdict

Six blind lanes produced 18 raw systems. The decision catalog retains 12 plus the shipped baseline.

The retained systems stay close to Misty Step while preserving meaningful range across calm ledgers, annotated folios, command cells, mechanical pressure keys, atelier frames, terminal atlases, and civic instruments.

Six proposals are archived, not deleted:

- `R2ANTH-1`: lower-datum duplicate; `R2RED-1` carries the Signal Loom identity better.
- `R2HALL-2`: its button family did not survive the rendered evidence surface.
- `R2IMP-1`: weaker quiet-ledger duplicate with an over-colored destructive treatment.
- `R2RED-2`: its keyed Civic Instrument concept was good, but the implementation collapsed and overlapped.
- `R2TASTE-3`: duplicates Quiet Atelier while rendering its signature frame less convincingly.
- `R2IMP-2`: exact Signal Register naming and command-cell overlap; `R2TASTE-2` has the stronger tactile pressure grammar.

Strongest current directions: `R2RED-3` Quiet Atelier, `R2MIN-2` Survey Folio, `R2TASTE-2` Signal Register, `R2ANTH-2` Control Folio, and `R2RED-1` Signal Loom.
